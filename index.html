How to play together

  • Sound on! The games talk, so no reading is needed. Tap 🔊 to hear it again, 🏠 to go back.
  • Levels: every game has 5 levels. Finish a level to unlock the next one and fill a dot on the game's button.
  • 🎈 Balloon Pop: listen for the letter and pop the balloons that show it. At level 3 the letter is hidden, so listen for the sound!
  • 🐶 First Sounds: say the picture's name slowly, stretching the first sound (“sssun”), then tap the letter it starts with.
  • 🃏 Letter Match: turn over two cards to find a capital letter and its small letter.
  • 🎵 Rhyme Time: say the words out loud. Which one sounds the same at the end?
  • 🧱 Build a Word: tap the letters in order as you hear each sound, then blend them: c‑a‑t → cat!
  • 🔢 Number Words: count the pictures, then spell the number word by tapping its letters in order. Say each letter name together.
  • 🦖 Feed the Dino: the dino asks for a sound. Feed it a picture that starts with that sound.
  • ✏️ Trace It: trace the big letter with a finger. Tap the sponge to start again.
  • 🚂 Alphabet Train: say the alphabet together and fill in the missing letters.
  • 📅 Days of the Week: the ending day is already written, so only the beginning is spelled: Mon‑day. Say the days in order together. At level 5 the day isn't named — work out which one comes next.
  • 🗓️ Days in Order: tap the days one after another to lay out the week. Count along the line as it fills. Later levels start on any day, so the week wraps round from Sunday to Monday.
  • 🔎 Which Word?: real reading. The words look alike — cat, hat, bat — so the child has to look carefully at the letters, not guess from the picture. The picture only appears once they are right. Level 5 changes just the vowel: pan, pin, pen.
  • 🎯 Middle Sound: the trickiest sound in a short word is the vowel in the middle. Say the word slowly together — c…a…t — and listen for the sound between the first and the last.
  • Tips: 5–10 minutes is plenty. Let your child tap, and praise trying, not just right answers.

Voice

Tip: for the most natural, child-friendly voices on a computer, open the games in Microsoft Edge. Phones and tablets already have good voices.

🔊 Sound check: hear every sound

Computer voices can only roughly say letter sounds. Open Sound check to hear every one, and to change any that come out wrong — each sound is a spelling the voice reads, so a different spelling fixes it. If a sound still isn't clear, say it yourself too. To use your own voice, record files named a.mp3, b.mp3… and put them in the audio/sounds folder.
