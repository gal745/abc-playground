How to play together

  • Sound on! The games talk, so no reading is needed. Tap 🔊 to hear it again, 🏠 to go back.
  • 🎈 Balloon Pop: listen for the letter and pop the balloons that show it. Say the sound together each time.
  • 🐶 First Sounds: say the picture's name slowly, stretching the first sound (“sssun”), then tap the letter it starts with.
  • 🃏 Letter Match: turn over two cards to find a capital letter and its small letter.
  • 🎵 Rhyme Time: say the words out loud. Which one sounds the same at the end?
  • 🧱 Build a Word: tap the letters in order as you hear each sound, then blend them: c‑a‑t → cat!
  • Tips: 5–10 minutes is plenty. Let your child tap, and praise trying, not just right answers. Younger children: start with Balloon Pop, Letter Match and Rhyme Time.

Voice

Tip: for the most natural, child-friendly voices on a computer, open the games in Microsoft Edge. Phones and tablets already have good voices.

Computer voices can only roughly say letter sounds. If a sound isn't clear, say it yourself too. To use your own voice, record files named a.mp3, b.mp3… and put them in the audio/sounds folder.
