For grown-ups: today's lesson, as one sheet to scroll down — b and d again, this time in words they already know: animals first, then food and toys, where the only question is the sound the word starts with and the picture does the rest. Part 4 leaves the screen for a real book read with you (ten minutes or so — the page goes quiet and waits, and the child taps when the book is finished). After it, words with a letter missing, then three- and four-letter words with a b or a d in them, read whole with nothing said first, then a long word taken a bit at a time, and a sentence that grows. Every instruction is spoken as the child reaches it, nothing is typed, and each part ticks itself off. The teacher-led worksheets are on the lesson sheets page.
