🎮 Go and play

For grown-ups: today's lesson, as one sheet to scroll down — space words they can sound out: moon, star, rock, flag, and the long ones, rocket, planet, comet, robot. They hear each one in its sounds, find the sound that has fallen out of a word, then build three words from a box of letters that holds every sound for all three at once — the same job as the new 🚀 Space Words game. Part 4 leaves the screen for a real book read with you (ten minutes or so — the page goes quiet and waits, and the child taps when the book is finished). After it: a quick b or d, words read whole with a picture to pick, a long word taken a bit at a time, and a sentence that grows. Every instruction is spoken as the child reaches it, nothing is typed, and each part ticks itself off. The teacher-led worksheets are on the lesson sheets page.
